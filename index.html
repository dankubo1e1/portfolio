1e1（イェル）

1e1（iel / イェル）は、東京を拠点に活動する音楽家・プロデューサー久保暖によるソロプロジェクト。

Hip-HopやR&Bといったブラックミュージックの文脈と、LA Beatをはじめとするクラブミュージックや先進的な電子音楽の意匠に、クラシックや現代音楽をルーツとする高度な音響生成技法・構造論を融合させ、独自のサウンドを展開する。

2018年に1st EP『Quartier Latin』で始動。2020年リリースの1st Album『Cryptobiosis』はWebメディア『Mastered』の企画にてプロデューサーSeiho氏の年間ベストにノミネートされるなど、シーンのクリエイターやメディアから深く支持される。
2024年には2nd EP『Asymptote』を発表。すべての作品は明確なコンセプトに基づいて精緻に構築され、言語と音楽、ポップネスと実験性の狭間を穿ち、思考と聴覚を揺さぶる体験を展開する。

その活動は楽曲制作にとどまらず、グラフィックやインスタレーションといった視覚・空間芸術にもアプローチを展開し、領域を限定しない実験と表現を続けている。
東京を中心に、全国のクラブやライブハウス、フェスでのライブパフォーマンスを行う。

Dan Kubo

Musician, artist, and producer based in Tokyo, Dan Kubo engages in a wide range of creative practices, centered around his solo project "1e1" (pronounced iel).

Utilizing proprietary sound-generation techniques and compositional approaches, he crafts immersive acoustic spaces that seamlessly bridge diverse musical genres—from the precise theoretical structures of classical and contemporary music to club music, including Hip-Hop, R&B, and LA Beat.

He has collaborated with numerous artists, including Seiho, Licaxxx, Ibuki Takai, Fuyuko Shioiri, Samayuzame, Kazuki Muraoka, and Pause Catti. Beyond collaboration, he provides music compositions and sound design for artists such as Toko Miura, haru., and Yuma Kishi, alongside scoring for films, media art, and TV commercials.

Kubo has also earned high acclaim in media arts and academia. His work was selected for the Jury Recommends list at the Japan Media Arts Festival, and he has received numerous awards at international competitions and academic conferences, including ICMC, NYCEMF, MUSLAB, and CCMC. In 2019, as part of the audio-visual duo "D+D" with Daisuke Kawashima, he performed Transposon Live at Spiral Hall in Omotesando, Tokyo.

M.A. in Music (Musical Creativity and Environment), Graduate School of Music, Tokyo University of the Arts

M.F.A. in Design (Spatial Design), Graduate School of Fine Arts, Tokyo University of the Arts

1e1

1e1 (iel, pronounced /jəl/) is a Tokyo-based solo project by musician and producer Dan Kubo.

The project presents a signature sound that fuses the contexts of black music—such as Hip-Hop and R&B—with club music and forward-thinking electronic music aesthetics like LA Beat, while integrating advanced sound-generation techniques and structural theories rooted in classical and contemporary music.

Launched in 2018 with the 1st EP Quartier Latin, 1e1 released its 1st album Cryptobiosis in October 2020, which was nominated for Producer Seiho’s Album of the Year on the web media outlet Mastered. In 2024, 1e1 released the 2nd EP Asymptote. Meticulously constructed around precise concepts, every work pierces the boundaries between language and music, pop sensibility and experimentation, offering an experience that engages both the mind and ear.

Extending beyond music production, 1e1 actively explores visual and spatial arts, including graphics and installation work, pursuing boundless experimentation. 1e1 performs live at clubs, live venues, and festivals across Japan, primarily centered in Tokyo.
