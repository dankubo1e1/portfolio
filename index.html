Dan Kubo / 1e1

Artist / Composer / Producer

1e1（イェル）は東京を中心に活動する音楽家・プロデューサーの久保暖によるソロプロジェクト。HipHop・R&Bを中心としたブラックミュージックに、現代音楽の一分野とされる電子音響音楽における独自の作曲手法を用いた音を織り交ぜた音楽を生み出す。

2018年10月31日に1st EP 「Quartier Latin」をリリース。各リリースは数多くのアーティストから支持を受け、2020年10月にリリースした1st album「Cryptobiosis」は、Webメディア『Mastered』にてSeihoが選ぶ当該年レコード大賞にノミネート。

その活動は楽曲制作にとどまらず、グラフィックやインスタレーションを主としたアート分野での制作も行なっており、Licaxxx、Kazuki Muraokaらとの共同制作も行なっている。

2024年8月14日、2nd EP「Asymptote」をリリース。

1e1 (pronounced "jəl") is a solo project by Dan Kubo, a musician and producer based in Tokyo. He creates music that combines mainly hip-hop and R&B, with sounds that use his own compositional techniques in acousmatic music, which is considered a branch of contemporary music.

He released his first EP, "Quartier Latin," on October 31, 2018. Each release has been supported by many artists, and his first album, "Cryptobiosis," released in October 2020, was nominated for the Record of the Year Award selected by Seiho on the web media "Mastered."

His activities are not limited to music production, but also include production in the art field, mainly graphics and installations, and he has collaborated with Licaxxx, Kazuki Muraoka, and others.

He released his second EP, "Asymptote," on August 14, 2024.
